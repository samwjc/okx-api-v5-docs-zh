余币宝接口迁移，新接口实盘已于2023/03/15上线，旧接口已于**2024/01/22**下线。

*   新增 [余币宝](/zh/rest-api-savings) 功能模块，原余币宝相关接口做如下调整
    *   [获取余币宝余额](/zh/financial-product-savings-get-saving-balance) 接口路径 `/api/v5/asset/saving-balance` 调整为 `/api/v5/finance/savings/balance`
    *   [余币宝申购/赎回](/zh/financial-product-savings-post-savings-purchase-redemption) 接口路径 `/api/v5/asset/purchase_redempt` 调整为 `/api/v5/finance/savings/purchase-redempt`
    *   [设置余币宝借贷利率](/zh/financial-product-savings-post-set-lending-rate) 接口路径 `/api/v5/asset/set-lending-rate` 调整为 `/api/v5/finance/savings/set-lending-rate`
    *   [获取余币宝出借明细](/zh/financial-product-savings-get-lending-history) 接口路径 `/api/v5/asset/lending-history` 调整为 `/api/v5/finance/savings/lending-history`
    *   [获取市场借贷信息（公共）](/zh/rest-api-savings-public-borrow-info-public) 接口路径 `/api/v5/asset/lending-rate-summary` 调整为 `/api/v5/finance/savings/lending-rate-summary`
    *   [获取市场借贷历史（公共）](/zh/financial-product-savings-get-public-borrow-history-public) 接口路径 `/api/v5/asset/lending-rate-history` 调整为 `/api/v5/finance/savings/lending-rate-history`
